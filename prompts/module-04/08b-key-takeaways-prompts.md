# Module 4 Key Takeaways Prompts

Use these prompts for review, retrieval practice, and transfer. Keep examples fictional, generic, synthetic, public, or explicitly authorized.

## 1. Five-takeaway recall

```text
Without looking at notes, state the five durable Module 4 takeaways.
For each, give:
- the decision rule;
- one safe example;
- one failure pattern; and
- one exam shortcut.
```

## 2. Delegation-first review

```text
Review the workflow below.

For every atomic step, identify:
- work performed;
- reversibility;
- stakes;
- accountability owner;
- side effect;
- delegation mode;
- validation;
- approval gate; and
- exception route.

Flag over-delegation and explain the smallest responsible correction.

Workflow:
[INSERT FICTIONAL WORKFLOW]
```

## 13. Integrated five-question review

```text
Review the workflow using these five questions:
1. Are requirements traceable and testable?
2. Are material numbers computed and reconciled?
3. Is each stage delegated according to risk and authority?
4. Are human review and irreversible actions operationally controlled?
5. Can the workflow be described accurately without overstatement?

Return:
- pass/fail for each question;
- evidence;
- unresolved gap;
- owner; and
- next action.
```

## 14. Scenario-generation drill

```text
Generate five original Associate-level workflow-integration scenarios.
Cover:
- ambiguous requirements;
- verified quantitative planning;
- prototype-to-infrastructure escalation;
- Delegation Mapping; and
- stakeholder communication.

For each scenario, provide four answer choices, the best answer, and why each distractor is weaker.
Do not reproduce remembered course or exam wording.
```

## 15. Oral certification review

```text
Ask me one Module 4 scenario at a time.
Require me to explain:
- the business outcome;
- the controlling risk;
- the correct delegation boundary;
- the required evidence or calculation;
- the human gate;
- the escalation condition; and
- the stakeholder message.

Score my reasoning, not keyword recall.
```

## Compact recall card

```text
Requirements → atomic, traceable, testable
Numbers      → execute and reconcile
Delegation   → reversibility + stakes + accountability
Authority    → human-retained
Side effects → approval before controlled execution
Prototype    → escalate when others depend on it
Messaging    → bounded tasks + evidence + limits + human gate
```
